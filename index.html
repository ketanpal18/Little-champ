Nurturing Young Minds Since 1999

At Little Champ Pre School, we believe that every child is unique and full of potential. Our play-based curriculum is designed to foster cognitive, social, and emotional development.

With a team of certified, passionate educators and a secure, stimulating environment, we lay the foundation for a lifelong love of learning.

Our Programs

Tailored learning experiences for every stage of early childhood development.

Toddlers (1.5 - 2.5 yrs)

Focus on sensory play, motor skills, and early socialization in a warm, supportive setting.

Pre-K (3 - 4 yrs)

Introduction to structured learning, creative arts, basic numeracy, and language development.

Kindergarten Prep (4 - 5 yrs)

Comprehensive preparation for elementary school, focusing on reading readiness, math, and independence.
